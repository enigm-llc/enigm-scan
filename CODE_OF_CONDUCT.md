# Code of Conduct

Enigm Scan follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
Everyone taking part in issues, pull requests and reviews is expected to
follow it.

Many people who come to this project fear they are being watched. Be patient
with them, never ask for their phone data, reports or personal details in
public, and point them to the private channels below when they need them.

To report unacceptable behaviour, write to the maintainers through
[enigm.io](https://enigm.io). Reports are handled in confidence. Security
issues go through [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.
